Where It Started

My journey through music started in high school when I began playing guitar with friends. What started as simply wanting to play guitar eventually became something much bigger. Music introduced me to performing, songwriting, recording, production, and many people who helped shape me into the musician I am today.

Learning Through Experience

Every stage of my life as a musician has taught me something different. Playing in bands taught me how to collaborate with other musicians, while performing taught me confidence and preparation. Later, learning music production gave me another way to understand and create music.

From my first band, Formal Intent, to my more recent experiences with Honey & Virosa, my musical journey has continued to change as I have learned new skills and taken on new roles.
